Print your ArUco marker

  1. Pick the marker size below, then hit Print (100% scale / no “fit to page”!).
  2. Verify with a ruler that the printed black square matches the chosen size.
  3. Place the marker flat on the same surface as the objects you want to measure.
  4. In the app, set Marker size to the same value.
